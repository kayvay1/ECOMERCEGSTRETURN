About this tool

Last updated · June 2026

🛍️ Meeso 📦 Flipkart 📮 Amazon

01 What it does

This is a free browser-based utility that converts the seller-reports you download from e-commerce marketplaces — Meeso's TCS sales/return files, Flipkart's GSTR-1 & GSTR-8 export — into a single, properly formatted GSTR-1 JSON file, ready to upload on the GST portal under Returns → Prepare Offline → Upload.

It handles the boring, error-prone parts by hand: mapping state names to POS codes, splitting tax into IGST vs CGST+SGST based on your home state, netting sales against returns, merging multiple marketplaces into one set of B2CS and HSN entries without creating portal-rejected duplicates, and building the supeco and doc_issue blocks the portal expects.

02 Who it's for

Small and medium sellers (and the accountants/CAs who file for them) who sell through Meeso, Flipkart, or similar marketplaces and currently build their GSTR-1 by hand in Excel every month or quarter.

03 How it works, technically

The whole app is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. When you upload an Excel file, your browser reads it locally using a JavaScript spreadsheet library — the file is never transmitted anywhere. The conversion math runs entirely on your device, and the final JSON is generated and downloaded directly to your computer.

your data, your device

Privacy Policy

Last updated · June 2026

Short version: nothing you upload ever leaves your browser. There's no server, no database, no analytics tracking your files, no account, and no copy kept anywhere by us.

01 What this tool does not do

  • It does not upload your Excel files, GSTIN, or generated JSON to any server.
  • It does not store your data after you close or refresh the tab.
  • It does not use cookies, third-party analytics, or advertising trackers.
  • It does not require you to sign in, register, or provide an email address.

02 How your data is actually processed

All file parsing, calculations, and JSON generation happen client-side using JavaScript running in your own browser. When you select or drag a file, it is read directly from your device's memory into the page — the bytes never travel over the network. Closing the tab clears everything; there is nothing to delete because nothing was ever stored externally.

03 Fonts and external resources

The page loads two things from the internet: web fonts from Google Fonts and a spreadsheet-parsing script (SheetJS) from a public code CDN, both purely to render the page and read Excel files. Your business data is never sent to either of these.

04 Your responsibility

Because everything runs locally, you are responsible for the security of the device and browser you use this tool on, and for keeping your downloaded GSTR-1 JSON and source Excel files safe afterward.

05 Changes to this policy

If this tool ever changes to involve a server or any form of data collection, this page will be updated to reflect that clearly before the change takes effect.
