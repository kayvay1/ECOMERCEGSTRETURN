step 01 · who's filing

Drop your GSTIN

This goes straight into the gstin field of your return. We also read its first two digits to detect your home state, so intra-state sales get taxed CGST+SGST automatically.

15 characters · first 2 digits = state code
step 02 · filing period

Monthly or quarterly?

Monthly returns need one month's data. Quarterly (QRMP) returns bundle three months — Apr–Jun is Q1, all the way to Jan–Mar as Q4.

Maps to GSTR-1 fp as MMYYYY
The fp filed is the quarter's last month, e.g. Q1 FY2026 → 062026
step 03 · the actual data

Which platforms are you filing for?

Pick every marketplace you sold on this period. We'll open the right upload boxes for each — and if you pick more than one, we automatically merge them into one clean GSTR-1 (no duplicate POS or HSN rows, like the portal requires).
